FAQ

Your questions, answered

Is teletherapy as effective as in-person sessions?

For many needs — speech and language, counselling, parent coaching, behaviour support and many exercise programmes — online sessions work very well. Your therapist will tell you if part of your care needs to be in person, and can refer you to a clinic or home-visit service nearby.

Who will I be matched with?

Our clinical team reviews your request and assigns a suitable licensed specialist — or a multidisciplinary team when that helps. You can also pick a professional yourself from the directory.

How do I know the professionals are qualified?

Every professional applies with their licence or registration, which our team reviews. Listings we have checked show a “Verified” badge.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Your booking details are only visible to you, the RehabVerve care team and the professional you book. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I join from outside Nigeria?

Yes — clients join from across Africa and abroad. Tell us your time zone in the booking notes.

How do payments work?

Our team confirms your plan and how to pay when we confirm your booking. There are no hidden fees.

What if I need to reschedule?

Message us or your therapist — continuity of your care is our priority. You can also cancel a request from My bookings.
