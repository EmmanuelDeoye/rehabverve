FAQ

Is it as good as in person?

For speech and language, counselling, parent coaching, behaviour support and many exercise programmes, online sessions work very well. If you need in-person care, your therapist refers you to a clinic or home-visit service nearby.

Who will I see?

Our clinical team matches you with a licensed specialist, or a team when that helps. You can also pick a professional yourself.

Are they qualified?

Every professional applies with their licence or registration. Listings we have checked show a “Verified” badge.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Only you, the RehabVerve care team and your professional see your booking. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I join from abroad?

Yes. Add your time zone in the booking notes.

How do I pay?

We confirm your plan and how to pay when we confirm your booking. No hidden fees.

Can I reschedule?

Yes — message us or your therapist, or cancel a request from My bookings.
