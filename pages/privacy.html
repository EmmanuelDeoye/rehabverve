Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

RehabVerve (“we”, “us”) runs a platform that connects patients, caregivers and families with rehabilitation professionals, rehab centers, mobile rehab services, teletherapy and online classes for children. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We aim to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the data-protection laws of the other countries where our users live.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, location and photo. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider (Google Firebase Authentication) and is never stored in our database.
  • Bookings and intakes: the service you request, who it is for, preferred dates, your contact details, any notes you add and your intake answers. These can include health information, which we treat as sensitive.
  • Children’s classes: when a parent or guardian enrols a child we collect the child’s first name and age, the parent’s contact details, learning notes, and the assignments the child hands in.
  • Professional, center and tutor listings: the details you publish (qualifications, fees, coverage areas, contact details, photo and CV) and the licence or certificates you send for review.
  • Reviews you post about professionals and centers, shown publicly with your name.
  • AI assessment: the details you enter (name or initials, age, gender, main concern) and your questionnaire answers are sent to our AI provider to create the questionnaire and summary. Saved assessments stay on your own device.
  • Usage counts: we count page views per day and per page, and clicks on sponsored notices. These counts contain no personal information. We also record whether a signed-in professional is online so their profile can show it.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and show you your bookings, classes and listing.
  • To match you with a professional, confirm bookings and contact you about them.
  • To review and verify professional, center and tutor applications.
  • To keep the platform safe — for example, preventing abuse and spam.
  • To improve our services using the anonymous usage counts.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who can see your information

  • The public: listings, reviews and class descriptions.
  • The professional or center you book: the booking details you send them.
  • Tutors: the enrolment details and assignments for their own classes.
  • The RehabVerve care and admin team: bookings, intakes, applications and enrolments, so we can support you.
  • Service providers who host or process data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage and cloud functions) and our AI model provider (accessed through GitHub Models). They process data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities where the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

If you continue a conversation on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply to those messages.

5. Children

Children should not create accounts. Classes and tutoring are booked by a parent or guardian, who must agree to this policy on the child’s behalf. We collect only what a tutor needs to teach the child. A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to see or delete their child’s information.

6. Security

Access to data is limited by database security rules — for example, bookings are visible only to the person who made them, the professional involved and our admin team. Uploaded licences and assignments are private. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

7. How long we keep data

We keep account data while your account is open. Booking records may be kept for up to six years for safety, quality and legal reasons. You can delete your account from Settings; we then delete your account details and, on request, your public listing.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. Contact us using the details below. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or your local data-protection authority.

9. Advertising

Sponsored notices on RehabVerve are chosen by our own team. We do not use third-party advertising networks or advertising trackers.

10. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date at the top.
